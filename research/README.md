# Research — Circle Packing and Generalized Branching

My research is in **circle packing**, a discrete analogue of conformal (analytic)
geometry. A *circle packing* is a configuration of circles with a prescribed pattern
of tangencies. By the **Koebe–Andreev–Thurston theorem**, every triangulation of a
surface is realized by a circle packing, unique up to Möbius transformation, and the
radii can be computed with the *Circle Packing Algorithm*. Circle packings give a
concrete, computable bridge between combinatorics and complex analysis — discrete
analytic functions arise as maps between packings.

My work develops the theory of **generalized branching** for such packings — extending
the notion of a branch point (where a discrete analytic map behaves like $z \mapsto z^k$)
to more general local configurations.
