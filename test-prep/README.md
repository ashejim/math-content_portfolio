# Test preparation — Praxis materials

Original test-preparation content I authored for students preparing for
teacher-licensure mathematics exams. All problems are my own; documents are
authored in LaTeX with figures drawn inline in TikZ/pgfplots.

## Praxis Core practice exam

[`praxis-core/`](praxis-core/) — a full-length practice exam for the
mathematics section of the **Praxis Core** (5732, teacher-licensure basic
skills), written for students I supported through WGU's math center — ~50
questions across the exam's content categories, with geometry figures and
data displays drawn in TikZ. PDF and `.tex` source included.

## Praxis 5003 practice exams

[`praxis-5003/`](praxis-5003/) — **two original full-length practice exams
modeled on the ETS Praxis 5003** (Elementary Education: Mathematics):
Version 1 (with a separate **answer-key PDF**) and Version 2, each with
`.tex` source. Writing parallel forms to a standardized exam's blueprint —
matched content categories, difficulty, and distractor design — is the
discipline behind my professional item-writing work.

*All materials are my original work, shared for portfolio purposes; they
reproduce no ETS content. Contact me before reuse.*
