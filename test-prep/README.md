# Test preparation — Praxis materials

Original test-preparation content I authored for students preparing for
teacher-licensure mathematics exams. All problems are my own; documents are
authored in LaTeX with figures drawn inline in TikZ/pgfplots.

## Praxis Core prep workbook

[`praxis-core/`](praxis-core/) — a prep workbook for the mathematics section
of the **Praxis Core** (teacher-licensure basic skills), written for students
I supported through WGU's math center: worked examples and practice across
the exam's content categories, with geometry figures and data displays drawn
in TikZ. PDF and `.tex` source included.

## Praxis 5003 practice exams

[`praxis-5003/`](praxis-5003/) — **original full-length practice exams
modeled on the ETS Praxis 5003** (Elementary Education: Mathematics, CKT):
two parallel forms, answer-choice-lettered variants for classroom use, and a
complete **answer key**. Writing parallel forms to a standardized exam's
blueprint — matched content categories, difficulty, and distractor design —
is the discipline behind my professional item-writing work.

*All materials are my original work, shared for portfolio purposes; they
reproduce no ETS content. Contact me before reuse.*
